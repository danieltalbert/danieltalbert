## How I work

<p align="center">
  <img src="./assets/sketch-to-system.png" alt="Two-panel illustration showing a rough whiteboard sketch becoming a clean diagram, then a structured presentation" width="100%">
</p>

I try to make the path from an early idea to a reviewed result visible:

1. Frame the question and its boundaries.
2. Turn the idea into a small, testable system.
3. Verify it with evidence that reflects real use.
4. Document what works, what does not, and what comes next.

## Engineering principles

- **Evaluation before optimization.** A strong score is only useful when the split, sample, and metric are honest.
- **Security is a product property.** Authentication, ownership, secrets, and failure behavior belong in the design.
- **Reproducibility is part of communication.** Tests, generated artifacts, and exact commands make claims inspectable.
- **Ambition needs boundaries.** I label prototypes, sandbox integrations, concept artwork, and future work clearly.
